Privacy policy

Gmail Invoice App is a personal tool with one user: its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

With read-only Gmail access, it searches the owner's own mailbox for supplier invoice emails and downloads their PDF invoices.

What it stores

It stores the owner's Google access token, and the invoices it needs, in the owner's own private cloud account. It stores no data about any other person.

What it shares

It sends the owner's invoices only to the owner's employer's expense system, at the owner's request. It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone else, and it does not use it for advertising.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
